Pot Wall Scanner

Scan a wheel-thrown bowl, mug or cylinder with your phone and see where the walls are thick and thin.

  1. Put the pot on a clear table with about 20 cm of space around it. A patterned cloth or newspaper under it helps the phone track.
  2. Look straight down into the pot and mark its centre.
  3. Walk the phone once round the outside, 30–40 cm away, then look down into the pot from every side.
